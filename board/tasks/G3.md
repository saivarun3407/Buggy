# G3 — A video and a screenshot for every proven bug

Epic: **E14** · Lane: **E** · Depends on: nothing

Read `board/AGENT_RULES.md` and `board/CONTRACTS.md` first.

## What

For each verified finding a browser recording shows the request, the payload and the target's real answer from the re-proof, saved with the logs under the engagement's proof folder.

## Done when

It runs on the real stack against the local practice apps, the tests and `make ci-lint` pass, and the board card says what was measured and what was not.
