# G1 — Buggy creates its own test accounts when the operator allows it

Epic: **E14** · Lane: **D** · Depends on: nothing

Read `board/AGENT_RULES.md` and `board/CONTRACTS.md` first.

## What

Find the sign-up form or API, register two accounts with operator-controlled email addresses, sign in, keep the sessions inside the gateway. Off unless the operator turns it on for the run. Stops with a clear reason on CAPTCHA, email verification or SSO.

## Done when

It runs on the real stack against the local practice apps, the tests and `make ci-lint` pass, and the board card says what was measured and what was not.
