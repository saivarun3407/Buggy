Read `board/AGENT_RULES.md` and `board/CONTRACTS.md` first.

## Why (who does this well, and what Buggy lacks)

RedAmon (101/104), PentestGPT (90/104), Cairn (54/54) and Hacker Bob (17 CVEs) have numbers. Buggy has none for its agents. This is the single biggest gap and it cannot be closed by writing code.

## Idea

With a model key: run both lab arms (with and without the pipeline) and the XBOW suite under the evidence contract from F3, then publish the scorecard and raw transcripts.

## Architecture

BLOCKED: needs model provider credentials, which this environment does not have and which only the owner can supply (`buggy onboard`). Everything else is ready once F3 and I3 are done:
`python -m benchmark.lab agent-run --arm with-pipeline`, `--arm without-pipeline`, the XBOW provider run, `python -m benchmark.evidence verify <dir>`, `python -m benchmark.scorecard <dir> --write`.
No number goes in the README until `verify` passes on the published directory.

Publishing anything that does not pass `verify`.

`benchmark.evidence verify` on the run directory.

A scorecard with raw transcripts that a third party can check.
