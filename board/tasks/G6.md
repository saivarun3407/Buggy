# G6 — Sign-up needs a person: Buggy nudges, the operator does one step, Buggy continues

Epic: **E14** · Lane: **D** · Depends on: G1

## What

On a CAPTCHA, email verification or a sign-up it cannot find, Buggy keeps the accounts it prepared, prints YOUR TURN with the exact step and signs in on --resume.

## Done when

It runs on the real stack against the local practice apps, the tests and `make ci-lint` pass, and the card says what was measured and what was not.
